# MOJIMO V2.2jp1

Play: https://aosugoi.github.io/mojimo-v2-2jp1/

English 🇬🇧 and Japanese 🇯🇵 public playtest edition. No sign-in or installation required.

Quest, 60-second mode, Study Hall and Lesson Trail choices include Japanese words with clean romaji readings. Only Quest and 60-second gameplay award coins. Quest gives 20 coins per perfect level of 10 correct answers. Timed runs give +2 per correct and −1 per wrong answer, with 0–100 coins awarded when the timer finishes; early exits give no timed coins. Learning modes save progress without coin rewards.

Includes all 19 characters and voices, Kana Garden, Dream Keys, the 600-pull guarantee, total pull counter, and key reward animation and chime. The standalone index.html embeds artwork and audio.

Progress stays in your browser, independent of V2.2i and other editions. Existing V2.2jp1 coins, keys, characters, scores and learning progress are preserved.
